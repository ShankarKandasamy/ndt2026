## Methods notes

- Clusters. F01 rows cluster by the interval-setting decision record id. Other families cluster by the line or the anchor record of the row. F08 and F10 slot-name clusters (F08:<slot>, F10:<slot>) hold one row each, so they are singletons. An F01 row and an F02 row on the same line are separate clusters.
- Draw. 90 rows from a pool of 170 non-dropped rows, seed 2026. Family quotas are proportional to pool size with largest-remainder rounding. Each family gets at least 4 rows, capped at its pool size. The rows needed to lift small families to the floor are taken one at a time from the family with the largest quota. As a result F02 drew 12 against a proportional 14.8; F07 drew 11 against a proportional 12.2. Within a family the draw is balanced across wording and language.
- Precision@k. The top k retrieved ids are mapped and deduplicated, then precision@k = hits / k, always k, even for a shorter list.
- Canonical map. A copy maps to its native record. A member of a Report revision chain maps to the chain head. A record whose register readings_from names a source survey maps to that source. Procedure, InspectionPlan and CorporateStandard revisions stay distinct. The same map serves retrieval and the language model.
- Raw-id exception. A row whose gold is not injective under the canonical map (two or more gold ids map to one canonical id) is scored on raw ids in both pipelines. Today these are F06-001, F06-003, F06-006 and F06-011, where the question is which copy or revision is which.
- id_depth universe. id_depth counts lookups over register ids and line tags, matched as substrings. iterated_id chains over a larger universe: it adds CML tags, report-chain base ids and instrument serials, and matches on word boundaries. A row labelled inf can therefore be reached by iterated_id.
- Negative half. It is 1 only if every cited record is gold-consistent; an empty answer scores 1. This is stricter than the FILLSPEC wording, which fails an answer only when it asserts a record that does not exist.
- F08 rows with gold NONE are scored under the negative half. They are not merged into the F12 abstention analysis, which keeps F12 rows only (n=5).
- Rows whose gold holds no register record (F01-022, F01-023, F07-011) are reported apart in both pipelines. They are excluded from the language-model headline and strata, as retrieval skips them, and listed in their own table.
- Small cells. A cell with n <= 5 or fewer than 3 clusters gives a coarse or degenerate interval (for example F03, 2 clusters). Such cells are marked †.
